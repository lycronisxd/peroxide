<h3>entry 2, 21/07/26 : 15:00 - 18:34</h3>
this took loads and loads of trial and error so strap in for it!<br \>
i first assigned footprints to all the components, which is all well and good, right?<br \>
wrong.<br \>
i hate 0805 footprints with my whole heart.<br \>
so after 5 redesigns trying to make all of the decoupling caps and what have you fit - i caved and used 0402 footprints; and oh my lord are they a godsend!<br \>
but after placing components and wiring them all up, i realised i forgot about the pin headers, and because i want the bottom as a solid ground plane - it meant that i had to rearrange everything in the schematic.<br \>
absolute nightmare.<br \>
