<h2 align="center">about.</h3>

<h3 align="center">
pentoxide is a small intro for me into the world of embedded electronics,<br \>
since i find it extremely interesting and its extremely powerful<br \>
for making pcbs without breaking out a devboard!<br \>
<br \>
the rp2040 seemed like a good place to start,<br \>
since the routing philosophy is similar in some aspects<br \>
to the atmega32u4 (which i have experience with)<br \>
but was also able to learn routing for components like<br \>
flash memory and voltage regulators.<br \>
</h3>
<hr>

<h2 align="center">features.</h3><br \>
<h3 align="left">
<ul>
<li>rp2040 based</li>
<li>16 gpio pins</li>
<li>12 digital only pins</li>
<li>4 analog+digital pins</li>
<li>5v</li>
<li>3v3</li>
<li>usb-c</li>
</ul>
</h4>
